some details abt the theme/appearance of the launcher:
this is a minimal minecraft launcher.
the main colors are "#f5de9a"-primary(usually for texts or buttons) "#182842"-secondary(usually for bg).
all elements are either sharp or have a simple rounded rectangle style having radius of 5px.
usually there are no hover colors to the interactive elements.
containers/spaces have no bg color for most of the time, but when they do, they use the black_26.
animations are minimal which includes fade in and fade out simultaneously.
there are no borders to elements except for some rare cases.
the ui looks modern and clean.
custom font "ps" for major elements(most of the time) and normal system font for other elements but making sure that it looks good.
custom icons/assets(most of the time) if they are not there, user provides them. he will decide- use flet lib icons or custom.
